# Launch a sustainable sneaker brand for runners who

## The Receipt

**Candidate names (unscreened):** Tally, Ledger Running Co., Onus, Fine Print, Gram & Kilo

### Strategy
The running brand that publishes the complete, audited footprint of every shoe it makes — proof before promises, for runners who read labels the way they read splits.

_Don't take our word for it. Read the receipt._

### Big idea
The Living Receipt: every pair ships with its audited, itemized footprint printed on the tongue and living at a public URL — and at mile 500 the receipt gets a second page: swap the outsole and midsole core with a rebuild kit and keep the same shoe on the books.
